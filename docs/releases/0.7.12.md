- App extensions of an IPA installed with `app install --container`, or IcliKit's `installIPAInContainer`, are found again. Each plug-in was registered at its path in the temporary copy of the IPA, which is deleted when the install returns, so LaunchServices kept no record of it: nothing could find or launch the extension, while the app itself ran. A packet tunnel provider never started, failing with `NEAgentErrorDomain Code=2` and no process or crash report. Plug-ins are now registered at their installed paths. Reported by UnownPkmn (Lakr233/vphone-cli#546).
- The install result's `plugins` lists the plug-ins found in the app, and `unregistered_plugins` those LaunchServices does not list after registration.
