- `app register`, `app refresh` and installs build the record uicache from uikittools-ng builds, roothide's fork for apps of a roothide bootstrap and Procursus' otherwise: the executable's entitlements and the containerization they ask for, the data and group containers, HOME and TMPDIR, and each plug-in with a container of its own. Before, a bootstrap app registered on iOS 27 lost its plug-ins, entitlements, app groups and data container. The record is then read back and compared field by field, and a registration LaunchServices did not keep in full fails. Contributed by @nekohasekai in #3.
- `app refresh` and `sb uicache` work like `uicache -a`: an app is registered again when its record differs from what uicache would register now, including a version-only change and a record an earlier registration left incomplete. A matching record is left alone, since registering an app stops it. A refresh after which a record still differs fails with `refresh_incomplete`, and the result lists Apple's apps and other installers' apps under the new `skipped` key.
- icli registers only apps of the bootstrap, roothide's `.jbroot-*` container included, and apps it installed in a container. It refuses Apple's apps, apps from the App Store or another installer, and a bundle whose identifier another installer's app already uses.
